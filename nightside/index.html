NIGHTSIDE

A tower defense on a turning planet. Your guns run on sunlight. The Blight only ever lands in the dark.

  • Sun sweeps, grid sags. Solar arrays only pay out on the lit half, and adjacent arrays boost each other — so a farm is a place, and the terminator will leave it. Capacitors carry that charge into the night.
  • Every tile is a frontier. Pods drop on the night side, then walk to the nearest Core Vent. There is no back row on a sphere.
  • Mortars need no power — but they cannot touch a flyer. Lasers can, until the grid browns out. Build both.

GRID LOST

HOW TO PLAY

Goal — survive 12 waves. Three Core Vents share one integrity pool; anything that reaches a vent takes a bite out of it.

Power — Solar Arrays produce only in daylight (the sun sweeps right across your base every 2 minutes), and each array touching another gains +22%. Clustering pays more but goes dark all at once; scattering is steady but weak. Capacitors store the surplus. When demand outruns supply your turrets slow down instead of dying — watch the power bar turn amber.

Alloy — Extractors sit on glowing ferrite seams only. Kills pay too.

Threats — Crawlers rush, Husks soak (armour halves beam damage), Drifters fly over your walls and mortars, Spitters stop to shell your buildings. Wave 12 brings a Leviathan.

Keys — 1–7 pick a structure, click a tile to place, U upgrade, X sell, space call the next wave early, P pause, M mute, Shift+R new planet.
