BOWL

A railway hump yard. Gravity sorts the freight train. You decide how hard to squeeze.

A locomotive shoves the train over a small hill. At the crest each cut comes free and rolls down into the bowl, a fan of eight sorting tracks. Line the switches, pinch speed off in the master and track retarders, and get every car to its outbound block coupling at walking pace: 6.4 km/h or less. Too fast damages freight. Too slow leaves a gap. Nothing can add speed back.
