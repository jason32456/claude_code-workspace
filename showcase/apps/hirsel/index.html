HIRSEL

A sheepdog trial. You stand at the post, the sheep are a field away, and all you can do is whistle.

D come bye: clockwise
A away to me: anticlockwise
W walk up · S lie down
Q steady · E get out
Space in here (shed) · F that'll do

The dog does exactly what it is told, a quarter of a second later. The sheep keep away from the dog, by more the faster it moves, and bunch up when they are frightened. To move them towards something, put the dog on the far side of them, stop it, and walk it on. The judge starts with every point and takes them away for going off line, missing gates and letting sheep break.
