MELTWATER

Shape the valley. Then release the melt and find out what you actually built.

The glacier melts on a timer and every drop obeys a shallow-water simulation — it finds the low line by itself, backs up behind what you leave in the way, and does not respawn if you send it the wrong way. You never touch the water. You only move dirt, and the spoil for a levee has to come out of a trench you chose the place of.

Mouse + keyboard. Best on a desktop browser.

How to play

The idea

Water runs downhill and conserves volume. Everything else follows from that: a channel dug a metre lower steals the flow, a filled basin spills from its lowest lip, and a dam does not stop water — it delays it, and the delay has to go somewhere.

Cut and fill

Digging adds to your spoil pile; raising spends it. Both count against earth moved, which is capped for the season — and costs triple once the melt is running.
