THE OTHER FIVE

You are one mind of nine. The other five are on their own.

One brain in the head, one in each arm. You can consciously attend to three arms at a time — the other five grip, probe and snatch on their own. They will find food you never saw. They will also reach into a crevice with a moray in it. Five nights on a reef where everything bigger than you eats things like you.

WASDcrawl — speed scales with planted arms
MOUSElook · aim the reticle
L-CLICKtask an arm at what you are aiming at
R-CLICKlet go — release every arm
SHIFTsqueeze — fit through gaps, halve your silhouette
Cfocus camouflage
SPACEjet — fast, anaerobic, loud
Qink — leaves a decoy shaped like you
Feat what you are holding

Squeeze, focus and each arm in transit all cost one of the same three attention pips. You cannot do everything at once — that is the game.
