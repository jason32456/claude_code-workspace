PLUMBLINE

The load is always late.

You are not moving a column. You are moving a trolley, and the column is forty metres below it on a cable, arriving wherever its own momentum says. Push the stick and the load stays behind. Let go and it catches up, and passes you.

The whole game is learning to stop on the beat. Brake exactly one full swing after you started — when the load has run out ahead of you and is coming back — and the sway dies in a single move. Brake half a swing early and you double it.

Everything you set is welded in where you actually put it. Sloppy floors lean, and the next floor is built on the one you left. The plumb gauge is what ends the run — that, and the sun going down.
