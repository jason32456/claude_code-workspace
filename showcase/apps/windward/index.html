WINDWARD

There is no throttle. You steer, you trim — the wind decides.

You cannot sail at it

A 36° cone dead upwind makes no drive at all. The mark is inside that cone. Zig-zag across the wind and pick the side that pays.

Trim or crawl

Angle of attack peaks 20° off the sail. Hold the green band on the sheet gauge — every turn changes where that band sits.

Read the water

Dark ruffled patches are more breeze, and they drift. Sailing to a gust beats sailing straight. Glassy patches are holes — avoid them.
