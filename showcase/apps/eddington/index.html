Nothing here is a shader trick: the bending, the shadow, the ring, the lopsided disk and the copies of the sky all come out of one equation, u″ = 3u² − u. Delete the 3u² and you have Newton, and everything below moves to exactly half.

The disk is a Shakura–Sunyaev thin disk, F ∝ r⁻³(1−√(r_in/r)), emitting isotropically in its own rest frame. What reaches you is g⁴F with the observed temperature gT, g the full redshift factor. Switch shading to no beaming to remove only the Doppler term and see what it was doing.

How far light bends, and the factor of two that made Eddington famous

A ray arriving from infinity with impact parameter b turns through α(b). Newton's gravity acting on corpuscles moving at c — the calculation Soldner published in 1801 — gives α = 2M/b. General relativity gives 4M/b in the same weak field, and diverges at b = 3√3 M where Newton's formula is still perfectly finite and perfectly wrong.

The photon ring, and why it is exactly e−π

Rays with b a hair above 3√3 M loop the hole before they leave, so the sky appears again and again in ever thinner rings pressed against the shadow. The spacing is not a fitted constant. Linearise the orbit equation about the photon sphere, u = 1/3 + δ, and the 3u² term gives δ″ = δ — one e-folding per radian of orbit, for every black hole that has ever existed. Each extra half-turn is π radians, so successive rings close on the shadow edge by e−π.

The dark patch is not the hole

Everyone calls it the event horizon. It is not: it is the set of directions whose rays end on the horizon, and it is 3√3 M across in impact parameter against a horizon at 2M. The bench measures it from the renderer, by bisecting on the camera's own viewing angle until the ray stops coming back.

Why one side is brighter

The inner disk orbits at a serious fraction of c, so the limb coming toward you is beamed and blueshifted and the limb going away is dimmed and reddened. Specific intensity over frequency cubed is a Lorentz invariant, so the bolometric brightness scales as g⁴ and a modest shift becomes a large brightness ratio.
