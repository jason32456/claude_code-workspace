LEYDEN

The lightning is not trying to kill you. It is trying to find the shortest way to the ground, and you are going to make sure that is through you.

The town of Leyden runs on charge somebody has to go up and fetch. You fly the Lure: a slow gas envelope with a copper streamer and a hull that starts to smell of ozone about four seconds after a good hit.

Drop the streamer under a charging cell and you become the likeliest thing in the valley. Take the strike, carry it down, dump it into a capacitor jar before the heat cooks you. Everything you don't catch goes into the powder mill.
