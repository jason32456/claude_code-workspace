Anamorph

Render a scene, throw the camera away, and take it back — the picture only resolves from one place.

What came back

Ground truth is held back until after the solver has committed. Every number here is measured now, not written into this page.

Five things this turned out to be about

1 · The easiest target is the one the textbook method cannot solve

Lay every marker flat and the general 3D→2D linear solve — one equation system for the whole camera matrix — loses rank, because a plane has no third dimension to constrain the third column of P. The homography route, which is only valid on a plane, sails through the case that destroys the general one.

working…

2 · Focal length and distance are the same thing — and relief is not what separates them

This project set out to show that a flat target cannot tell a long lens far away from a short lens close up, and that scene relief is what breaks the tie. The first half is true and the second half is not. The measurement below is of the thing that actually decides it.

3 · The claim that did not survive

The relief sweep that was supposed to produce finding 2. It is kept because it is the measurement that overturned the plan, and a page that only shows the experiments that worked is not showing its work.

4 · Two free parameters that always help, and buy nothing

Render a true pinhole — no distortion at all — then fit it with the two radial coefficients free. They have nothing real to find, so anything they do is absorbing noise. The residual must fall. The question this answers is whether the camera gets better.

working…

5 · Spread beats count

One photograph of twelve markers, used twice: six chosen spread across the frame, six chosen from one corner. Same image, same marker size, same count, same noise. Only where they sit differs.

Checks against answers derived somewhere else

A pose pipeline that is subtly wrong is still perfectly self-consistent: the residual would be small, every panel would look right, and the camera would be wrong. These are the things that would catch that.
