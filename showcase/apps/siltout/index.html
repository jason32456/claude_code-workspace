SILTOUT

The line is the only way home. The silt is all your fault.

How to dive
  • Tie your primary on the orange-flagged knob in the daylight basin (E). From then on the reel pays out line straight from your last tie-off to you.
  • Tie off at bends. A line that cuts across rock chafes, and after a few seconds it parts. Rock knobs on the walls are tie-off points.
  • Frog kick (W) is gentle. Flutter (Shift+W) is fast, burns gas and blasts silt off any floor within 3 m. Silt hangs, spreads and drifts with the flow.
  • Trim. Gas in your wing compresses as you go deeper and expands as you rise (Boyle). R adds gas, F dumps it. Touching the floor counts as a contact and kicks up a cloud.
  • Thirds. Turn round by the time you've used a third of your gas. Deeper means faster breathing.
  • Out. Swim back to each tie-off and untie it (Q), or the line will wrap the corners behind you. If you can't see, hold G: touch contact slides you along the line, and W moves you toward home.
