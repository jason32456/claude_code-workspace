GATHER

A hot glass shop. Five orders. One gather each.

The glass is always cooling. Below 620°C it will not move, and a tool will crack it. F puts it back in the fire — hold longer to push deeper and reheat more of it.

Always be rolling. Gravity pulls on the piece in its own turning frame, so spin cancels it and stopping does not. A piece that droops off its axis scores badly, and far enough falls off the pipe.

Breath goes where the glass is thinnest and hottest, not where you point. To move the bubble, chill the part you want to keep.

The shears are one-way. Cutting the tip open ends all blowing — after that the only shaping force left is spin.

Sound on. M mutes.
