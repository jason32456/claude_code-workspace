EMBERLINE

Retardant does not put fire out. It makes ground it cannot cross.
Fly the line where the fire will be.

The fire

A cellular burn model with fuel, moisture, slope and wind. It runs uphill and downwind exponentially, throws embers over your lines, and goes out only when every edge of the burn is on ground it cannot cross.

The aircraft

6000 litres — four seconds of continuous drop. Low and slow paints a dense, narrow, effective line; high paints a wide useless smear. Refill by skimming the lake under 40 m at under 62 m/s.

Winning

Keep the buildings. Cut the head off before it reaches them, and hold the flanks with water, rock and old burn. Containment is a fact, not a score.
