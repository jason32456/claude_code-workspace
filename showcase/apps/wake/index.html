The bench

The solver run against answers it did not produce: closed-form channel flows, Ghia's cavity table, the Schäfer–Turek cylinder benchmark and Roshko's shedding law. Each row shows what was expected, what came out, and whether it passed. The long ones take a minute in a worker.

—

    D2Q9 lattice-Boltzmann, BGK collision, Guo forcing, halfway bounce-back, momentum-exchange forces. No libraries, no build step, no GPU compute: the solver is plain typed arrays and runs unchanged in the page, in the worker and under Node (node tests.mjs).
