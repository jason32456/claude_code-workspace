Penumbra uses sleep's natural memory consolidation to help what you study stick.
Sessions are capped and designed to end — not to keep you up.

What the science supports: studying close to sleep improves retention because there's no subsequent waking activity to interfere. We use FSRS spaced repetition with a sleep-bracket bias. We don't claim learning happens during sleep itself.
