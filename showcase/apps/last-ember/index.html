LAST EMBER

Your torch is the only thing standing between you and the dark.

Descend eight floors of a forgotten mine. Every turn burns fuel; every step you take is a step you can see, until you can't. Bump monsters to fight them, drink potions to survive, and find the Emberheart before your last torch gutters out for good.
