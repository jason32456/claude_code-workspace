Run the self-test

The FFT has an exact oracle — an independently written naive DFT — and the MIDI writer has one too, a reader that parses its own output back. Both are checked here rather than assumed.

The textbook fix makes it worse. Harmonic summation asks each pitch to collect its own overtones — but that runs the leak backwards. Salience at A collects the energy at E×3, so a played E donates to A, and a C major chord grows a phantom A: exactly the A minor confusion the technique was meant to prevent. It ships here as a toggle, off by default, with its invented pitch classes drawn in red.

Measured on synthesized audio. Accuracy is reported only where ground truth exists. Drop a file or use the microphone and the number disappears rather than being invented — those are signals this app has never seen, which is the whole reason they are here.
