Waggle

You are a scout honeybee. Find the flowers, fly home, and tell your sisters where they are with the waggle dance, in the dark on a vertical comb: the angle from straight up is the angle from the sun, and every second you waggle is 250 metres.

How to play

Flying  AD turn (or click to steer with the mouse) · W faster · S slow · Space climb · C dive · Shift boost · V camera · hold Tab for 4× time

Tasting  Drop below 6 m and slow down over a flower patch. One second of sipping records its sweetness and its bearing and distance from home. It also refills your crop.

Dancing  Fly into the hive entrance (or press E near it). Aim with the mouse and hold the button or Space to waggle. The crowd jostles you, so keep steering. Bees watching you average 3–5 runs and then fly what you said.

The sun moves. Inside the hive you only know where it was when you came in. Near noon it moves fast. Leave and come back to look again.

Recruits who find flowers forage, come home, and dance too. Patches run dry and regrow. Some only bloom in the morning. Some are poisoned.
