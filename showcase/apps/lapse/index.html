A balloon competition at dawn. There is no rudder and no engine. All you have is a burner, a vent, and wind that blows a different way at every height. To go somewhere, find the layer that is already going there.

How to fly

Up and down  Hold Space to burn. Hot air is lighter, and you rise about half a minute later. X or Shift opens the vent and dumps heat. Watch the vario, not your nerves: a balloon carries several tonnes of air and turns slowly. H holds the current height for you, and Q/E move it 25 m.

Steering  The wind ladder shows the wind at each height, measured by the pibals (little white balloons) going up from the launch field. Green arrows point at the goal. Chimney smoke shows the surface wind, and the other balloons show theirs.

Goals  Over a cross, press M to throw a marker. It falls through every layer below you and drifts in each. 1000 points at the centre, nothing beyond 500 m. A ◇ waltz lets you choose any one of its crosses.

Landing  Before the thermals start, put down in pasture, stubble or fallow. Crops, livestock, woods and water cost points. Never touch a power line. Hold Tab (or F) to speed time up on long drifts.
