Is it actually knotted?

Pull a cable out of the drawer and find out it is not knotted — and that the question was not well-posed until you decided how to close it.

The difficulty. Knot type is an invariant of embeddings of the circle. A cable is an arc, and every open arc in space can be pulled straight without cutting it — so every open cable is unknotted, and the question has the same answer for all of them. To get a non-trivial answer at all you must first close the arc, and the closure is a choice you are making rather than a fact about the cable. This office therefore certifies closures, not cables.

The collapse

Deleting a vertex whose triangle the rest of the cable stays clear of is an ambient isotopy, so the knot cannot change. Watch the tangle lose vertices while the polynomial refuses to move. Reaching a triangle is a proof of unknottedness that no polynomial was consulted for.

Which way did you close it?

The same open cable, closed toward infinity in many directions. Each closure is a genuine knot with a genuine type. If the histogram has more than one bar, the cable does not have a knot type — your closure does.
