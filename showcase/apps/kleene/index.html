A wrong equivalence verdict is the one failure this app cannot tolerate, so the decision procedure is checked in-page against pairs whose answer is known independently.

Whole-string semantics. A pattern matches a subject when it matches all of it, which is the semantics under which automaton equivalence is meaningful.

No backreferences, no lookaround — deliberately. (a+)\1 is not a regular language, so no finite automaton recognises it and none of the machinery here applies to it. That exclusion is the argument, not a shortcut.
