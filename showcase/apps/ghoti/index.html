ghoti

gh as in tough, o as in women, ti as in nation. Therefore ghoti spells fish — the oldest joke about English spelling. This page learns to read English aloud from 117,493 pronunciations, with nobody ever saying which letter makes which sound, and then asks the dictionary whether the joke is true.

It is not. Everything below is measured in your browser, right now, from scratch.

Read it aloud

Type anything — a real word, a surname, a word that does not exist. The model has never been told a single spelling rule. Press play and listen: a pronunciation it got right sounds like the word, and one it got wrong is wrong in a way you do not need a number to grade.

The trial

Every occurrence of each substitution is found in all 117,493 words, together with where in the word it happens — which is the part the joke leaves out. A substitution that is only ever legal in one position cannot be borrowed for a position it never occupies.

The spelling system of English

Nobody wrote this table. It is what the aligner concluded after looking at the dictionary, with no supervision: for every letter, the probability that it spells each sound. Brighter is more probable. The right-hand column is silence — the letter contributing nothing, which English does a great deal of.

What it discovered

The dictionary says knight → N AY1 T and never says which letters made which sounds. Six letters, three sounds, no correspondence given. These are recovered, not given.

How much context helps, and where it stops helping

The same counts, decoded with progressively wider windows of surrounding letters. More context should keep helping right up until the contexts get so specific that they are memorising training words instead of generalising.

The aligner converging

EM's log-likelihood is guaranteed by theorem to never decrease. That makes it a genuine correctness check rather than a progress bar: a dip here is a bug, and the suite asserts it.

Checks
